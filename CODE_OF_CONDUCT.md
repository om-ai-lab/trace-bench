# Code of conduct / 行为准则

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss evidence and technical choices;
do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or disclose another person's private
information. Assume contributors have different backgrounds and experience.

保持尊重和建设性，围绕证据与技术选择讨论。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁或泄露他人隐私。
尊重贡献者不同的背景与经验。

Maintainers may edit/remove violating content and limit participation.
For concerns, request a private maintainer contact through an issue without
disclosing sensitive details. Maintainers will review context and respond
proportionately.

维护者可编辑/删除违规内容并限制参与。遇到问题时，可通过 issue 请求维护者提供私密
联系渠道，不要公开敏感细节。维护者将结合上下文审查并采取适当措施。
