# Data terms

English | [简体中文](DATA_TERMS.zh-CN.md)

For the modified StreamingBench annotations in this repository, the
StreamingBench authors have granted permission to redistribute the modified
annotation files. Annotations newly created by TRACE as part of that permitted
StreamingBench work are released under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/),
as permitted by that arrangement. This requires attribution,
noncommercial use and sharing adaptations under the same license.
The [MIT license](LICENSE) applies to TRACE's own code, not the data.

Questions, options, answers and other source-derived fields remain subject
to their upstream terms. TRACE additions do not relicense that content.
Source videos and model weights are not included; obtain them from their providers.

## Upstream permissions

- [StreamingBench](https://github.com/THUNLP-MT/StreamingBench): the authors
  granted permission to redistribute the modified annotation files in this
  repository, and permitted newly created annotations to be released under CC
  BY-NC-SA 4.0. This permission does not cover the original videos or other
  upstream source data.
- [OVO-Bench](https://github.com/joeleelyf/ovo-bench): its documentation states
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, while its dataset metadata has stated CC BY-SA 4.0.
  The applicable annotation license is awaiting author clarification.

Because the combined release also includes OVO-Bench annotations whose terms
are unresolved, its manifest remains marked `private_provisional` and new runs
remain provisional. This status does not override the StreamingBench authors'
permission or grant rights over unresolved upstream content.

Cite TRACE and the upstream papers, identify modifications and retain applicable
notices. See [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
